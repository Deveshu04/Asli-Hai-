How it works

Two voice agents on Bolna and a small backend. The parent never hangs up.

  1. The parent taps one button

    Before paying anyone who claims to be family or police, they press the yellow button saved on their phone.

  2. Saathi calls them back

    In two questions she finds out who called, what they wanted and how much.

  3. A second call checks the family

    While the parent waits, another agent calls the family member with one yes-or-no question. A yes also needs the family word.

  4. The verdict lands in the live call

    The answer is pushed straight into the parent's call, and the family gets a WhatsApp summary.

Built so it can't be talked into a wrong answer

Fixed safety wording

Every verdict is a fixed line. The AI never improvises whether a call was real.

The family word never reaches the AI

It isn't in any prompt or log. Only the server compares it, in English letters or the family's own script.

A “no” is enough to stop a payment

If the family member didn't call, that settles it. A “yes” unlocks money, so it also needs the family word.

Never left waiting

Unanswered calls move to the next family member. After 75 seconds without a clear answer, Saathi tells the parent not to pay until they reach the family themselves.

Police and CBI calls get a straight answer

There is no such thing as a “digital arrest”. Saathi says so, tells the parent to hang up, and reads out 1930 if money already went.

In the family's own language

Saathi listens and speaks in 12 languages through Sarvam, chosen when the family registers. In Hindi the same agents also run on Deepgram and Cartesia, so drills measure which is faster and more accurate.

Saathi speaks the family's language

Twelve languages, picked when the family registers. Every call opens in that language, and every fixed safety line is written and voiced in each one.

For families

A son or daughter sets it up once for their parent.

  1. Register the parent, one or two family members to call, and a family word.
  2. Open the button link on the parent's phone and add it to the home screen.
  3. Tell them: before paying anyone who calls asking for money, press the yellow button.
